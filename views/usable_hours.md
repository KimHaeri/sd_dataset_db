# Usable hours

Generated by `tools/views.py` from `usable_as`, `hours_reported` (or `hours_measured` when no stated figure exists) and `data/splits.csv`; do not edit. Hours are recording hours as stated by the sources (speech-only hours are lower); "derived" rows are computed from session shares or totals (see `views/usable_hours.csv`, column `basis`). Survey leads and datasets on hold are not counted. Simulated data (`speech_style` simulated_mixture: mixtures built from other corpora, and LibriCSS, a room replay of LibriSpeech) is totalled apart from real recordings.
